# Privacy Policy — Rightmove Bulk Scraper

This extension collects no data, and nothing you scrape is sent anywhere except your own device.

It runs entirely in your browser. The only network requests it makes are:

- **rightmove.co.uk** — to read the search results and listing pages you're viewing, so they can be compiled into a spreadsheet.
- **media.rightmove.co.uk** — to fetch a floorplan image, only if you turn on the optional "Estimate size from floorplan" feature. The image is read on-device using OCR; it is never uploaded anywhere.
- **api.postcodes.io** — a free, public UK postcode-lookup service, only if you enter a postcode into the optional "Distance from" field, to convert it to coordinates for the distance column.

No listing data, postcode, or anything else you enter is sent to the developer or to any third party beyond those direct, user-triggered requests. Your slider weights, postcode, and settings are saved only in your own browser's local storage, so they're remembered between uses — they never leave your device.

This is an independent, unofficial tool and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Rightmove.
